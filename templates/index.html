How to spot a phishing link

Check the domain

The real site name is just before the first “/”. paypal.com.evil.xyz belongs to evil.xyz.

Urgent language

“Verify now”, “account suspended” and “KYC update” are common lures.

IP addresses and “@”

Real companies do not use raw IPs or hide hosts behind “@”.

Never share OTP/PIN

No bank or app will ask for them through a link.
